### Note structure

1. **Meaning and purpose:** explain what the concept means and when it helps.
2. **Prerequisites:** list the few skills needed; briefly teach a missing basic before continuing.
3. **How to recognise it:** show the useful clues without promising recognition in a fixed number of seconds.
4. **Method:** show the standard steps and explain why they work. For Maths, show each calculation; for English, explain the sentence function and context.
5. **Worked examples:** begin with one easy example. Add a contrasting example when needed. Mark examples as original unless their PYQ source is known.
6. **Rules, limits and exceptions:** give formulas with defined symbols/units, or grammar rules with valid examples. Avoid "always/never" claims unless justified within the stated scope.
7. **Common mistakes:** explain the wrong approach and its correction. Do not invent claims about how often an examiner uses a trap.
8. **Short recall note:** a few useful rules/formulas and one reminder to check. Include a clear diagram for visual/spatial topics.
9. **Sources and uncertainties:** identify supplied material and reliable references for corrections. Mark unresolved facts explicitly.

### Practice and progress

- Reference notes contain worked examples, not a large question bank. Keep independent practice and its results separate.
- When teaching interactively, ask **one question at a time** and wait for my attempt before showing the solution.
- Progress from concept/examples to unaided easy questions. Reach **70% on an easy set** before harder Tier-II/CBT-2 PYQs; then add timed practice.
- Distinguish guided, independent, timed and retention attempts. Do not mark a chapter mastered because I read or copied the notes.
- Log errors and re-solve them after **7 and 21 days**, without notes. Use the master plan's existing blocks; add no study hours.

### Accuracy rules for the generated notes

- Do not invent question weightage, frequency labels, official cutoffs or guaranteed scores.
- State an exam pattern, marking rule or skill requirement only with the applicable official notice and cycle. Older notices are preparation baselines, not confirmed 2027 rules.
- Set timing from the exam format and measured practice. There is no universal 15-second skip rule for every subject or learner.
- Date current-affairs facts and other changeable information. Separate source content from added explanations and corrections.
- Never claim to have read a PDF, checked a notice or verified a PYQ unless that actually happened.
