### 🧠 Reasoning Protocol: 2 Days Normal, 3 Days Max for Tough Topics

| Study Session | Core Work & Focus | Passing Gate Before Advancing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1 — Understand** | Concepts, attempted worked examples, then independent basic questions. Start suitable PYQs as soon as ready. | Explain the method and solve $\ge$ 70% of a fresh basic set unaided. |
| **Day 2 — Apply** | Different question patterns, representative PYQs, a short topic test, and corrections. | Record accuracy, assisted answers, slow patterns, and remaining gaps. |
| **Day 3 — Tough Topics Only** | Repair difficult parts, attempt fresh PYQs, and retest. | Record whether the remaining issue is understanding, accuracy, or speed. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Hard Cut-Off Rule:**
> - **Two mornings (4 hours)** is the normal allocation; **three mornings (6 hours maximum)** is the strict cap for tough topics.
> - **Split combined topics before starting:** Calendars vs. Clocks; Ranking vs. Linear Seating vs. Circular Seating vs. Puzzles; R18 supplements.
> - **Cut-Off at Day 3:** If you are still struggling or slow after Day 3, **DO NOT take a 4th morning.** Advance to the next topic on Day 4. Flag the weak subtopic as Red/Amber and transfer it to the **Sunday 2-hour repair slot**.
> - Basic conceptual gaps remain marked **unfinished**; do not claim completion merely because 3 days elapsed.

> 3. **Week 15 Mid-Way Buffer (Feb 8–14):** Mid-point diagnostic and gap repair across Arithmetic (`M01`–`M14`), Reasoning exit tests, and General Science. No new topics introduced.
> 4. **English Morning Sprint (Weeks 16–28):** 13 full weeks (78 mornings = 156 hours). Grammar foundations and error spotting lock down first, followed by voice/narration, vocab retention, and reading comprehension.
> 5. **Sunday Reasoning Maintenance (Weeks 16–30):** While English commands weekday mornings, Reasoning is actively maintained via a **45-minute mixed test + error review** inside the Sunday test allocation.
> 6. **Weeks 29–30 Final Consolidation (May 17–31):** Full-syllabus diagnostics, error notebook cleanup, peak mock analysis, and typing benchmarks leading directly up to the May 31 readiness milestone.

> **Solve questions each time—never just passively re-read notes.** A topic is *not* revised until you can retrieve and solve its problems cold without notes or video hints.

### 🔄 The 5-Stage Learning Cycle

> $$\text{Video Course (Learn)} \longrightarrow \text{PYQ Book (Practise)} \longrightarrow \text{Timed Test (Audit)} \longrightarrow \text{Error Log (Repair)}$$

```text
 ┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐
 │  1. Understand  │ ──> │ 2. Build Basics │ ──> │ 3. Apply to PYQ │ ──> │ 4. Build Speed  │ ──> │    5. Retain    │
 │ (Video/Concept) │     │ (10-15 Easy Qs) │     │ (Tier-II PYQs)  │     │  (Timed Tests)  │     │ (7/21-Day Fix)  │
 └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘     └─────────────────┘
```

| Stage | Actionable Execution | Advancing Gate / Standard |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Understand** | Watch short video lesson; pause and attempt worked examples before the instructor. | Explain the core method in plain words without replaying the video. |
| **2. Build Basics** | Solve 10–15 easy textbook questions without a timer; write down clear steps. | Achieve $\ge$ 70% accuracy independently before touching hard questions. |
| **3. Apply to PYQs** | Move to moderate and harder CGL Tier-II / NTPC CBT-2 PYQs. Attempt before viewing solutions. | Solve a mixed PYQ set independently; mark any assisted answer as "Assisted". |
| **4. Build Speed** | Practice short timed sets (e.g., 10 questions in 8–10 minutes) and take weekly chapter tests. | Maintain $\ge$ 80% accuracy under time pressure; analyze skipped/slow items. |
| **5. Retain** | Re-solve errors in your notebook at **Day 7** and **Day 21** with zero notes. | Both re-solves 100% correct without hints; otherwise reset cycle. |

> [!TIP]
> **Tamil-Medium Guidance:** Use Tamil explanations to master tricky underlying concepts, while recording English mathematical and grammatical terminology in your notes. When stuck: make an honest 3-minute attempt $\to$ inspect only the specific step that blocked you $\to$ close the solution $\to$ re-solve completely.

### 📓 Note-Taking System (3-Part Structure)

Organize your notebook or digital folder into three distinct, non-overlapping sections:

| Section | Exact Purpose & Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| 📘 **1. Topic Notes** | Meaning, exact formula/rule, when it applies, 1 clean worked example, 1 common trap. |
| 📕 **2. Error Notebook** | Book/page/question number, root cause of error, corrected method, Day 7 & 21 review logs. |
| 📒 **3. Quick Revision** | High-yield formulas, grammar exceptions, idioms/vocab, and tricky static GK facts. |

#### Example Note Format:

```text
Topic: Percentage (M08)
Meaning: Fraction out of 100.
Rule: p% of Quantity = (p / 100) × Quantity.
Example: 15% of 200 = (15 / 100) × 200 = 30.
Trap: Always verify which quantity represents the 100% base value.

Error Reference: Pinnacle PYQ 4th Ed, Page 142, Q28
My Mistake: Took final price as base instead of original price.
Correct Method: Base = 500, Increase = 50 -> % Increase = (50/500)*100 = 10%.
Review Schedule: [Day 7: Passed clean] | [Day 21: Due Nov 24]
```

### ⚠️ The Error Analysis & Recovery Rules

1. **Error Categorization:** For every wrong or skipped question, categorize the root cause:
   - 🧠 *Concept Gap* | 🧮 *Calculation Slip* | 👓 *Misread Question* | ⏱ *Time Pressure* | 🎲 *Wild Guess*
2. **Double Spaced Re-Solve:** Re-solve independently around **Day 7** and **Day 21**. For Reasoning during the English phase, use the next Sunday review slot and record the actual date. Remove the question only after both re-solves are correct without hints.
3. **Sub-70% Score Protocol:** Identify the cause first. Allocate two targeted repair sessions inside the subject's correction time or Sunday repair allocation. After the Reasoning two-/three-day first pass, move speed/occasional-error work to weekends; unresolved basic methods remain unfinished and block dependent topics. Preserve evening Maths and the current morning phase; do not add daily blocks for every subject.
4. **Bad Day Routine:** When exhausted or disrupted, execute the emergency maintenance routine:  
   `20 min Maths` + `15 min current morning subject (Reasoning or English)` + `15 min GA` + `10 min Typing` = **60 min total**.  
   Log it honestly as a recovery day; resume normal routine tomorrow without doubling hours.
