## 1. 🧭 Strategic Purpose & Habit Architecture

Studying four full subjects every day while working roughly **8 AM–7 PM** fractures mental focus and reduces learning depth. This foundation routine solves that problem by decoupling **rapid factual recall** from **deep chapter problem solving**:

- **The Morning & Evening Depth Model:** 90 minutes of dedicated morning chapter study + 180 minutes of uninterrupted evening Maths depth.
- **The Foundation Habits:** Short, non-fatiguing daily speed drills (Maths 25m, English 30m, GA 10m on the job) build subconscious fluency and eliminate calculation friction.
- **The Golden Habit Rule:** Attempt from memory (closed-book) ──► Check answers ──► Repair errors immediately ──► Re-test spaced over time.

> **தமிழில்:** தினமும் எல்லாப் பாடங்களிலும் புதிய அத்தியாயம் படிக்க வேண்டியதில்லை. அடிப்படை நினைவாற்றல் பயிற்சியைச் சிறிய நேரத்தில் செய்து, முக்கியப் பாடத்திற்கு ஆழமான நேரத்தைப் பாதுகாப்பதே நோக்கம்.

### 3.3 Concrete Checklist: The 5 Core Calculation Drills (8:00 – 8:25 PM)

Execute these five 5-minute modules daily in strict sequence using closed-book retrieval:

| Drill # | Module Name | Daily Scope & Targets | Execution Standard |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Shuffled Tables (1–30)**<br>*(5 min)* | • Mixed tables: **12× through 30×**.<br>• Example prompts: $19 \times 7 = 133$, $23 \times 8 = 184$, $28 \times 6 = 168$, $247 \div 13 = 19$. | Random shuffled prompts (never recite $19 \times 1, 19 \times 2$ in order). Recall product in $< 2$ seconds without manual column addition. |
| **02** | **Squares ($1-50$) & Cubes ($1-30$)**<br>*(5 min)* | • **Squares 1–50:** $24^2 = 576$, $37^2 = 1369$, $43^2 = 1849$, $47^2 = 2209$.<br>• **Cubes 1–30:** $12^3 = 1728$, $15^3 = 3375$, $17^3 = 4913$, $21^3 = 9261$.<br>• **Roots:** $\sqrt{1444} = 38$, $\sqrt[3]{2744} = 14$. | Bidirectional recall: $1369 \to 37$, $1728 \to 12$. Unit digit signatures ($7^2 \to 9, 8^3 \to 2$). Precision: $\sqrt{625} = 25$; solving $x^2 = 625$ gives $x = \pm 25$. |
| **03** | **Reciprocal Fractions to % ($1/1$ to $1/20$)**<br>*(5 min)* | • Unit fractions $1/2$ to $1/20$ & key multiples ($3/8 = 37.5\%$, $5/6 = 83\frac{1}{3}\%$, $4/7 \approx 57.14\%$).<br>• Application: $12.5\%$ of $640 = 80$, $16\frac{2}{3}\%$ of $420 = 70$. | Instant fractional substitution. Never compute $12.5 \times 640 / 100$; immediately calculate $\frac{1}{8} \times 640 = 80$. |
| **04** | **Pythagorean Triplets & Geometry**<br>*(5 min)* | • **Base Triplets:** $(3,4,5), (5,12,13), (7,24,25), (8,15,17), (9,40,41), (11,60,61), (12,35,37), (20,21,29)$.<br>• **Scaled Multiples:** $(15,36,39), (14,48,50), (16,30,34)$.<br>• **Mensuration Multiples:** $\pi \approx 22/7$, radius $r=7 \to \text{Circumference} = 44, \text{Area} = 154$; $r=14 \to C=88, A=616$. | Spot hypotenuse/legs in 1 second without applying $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. Saves 45 seconds per Mensuration/Trigonometry question. |
| **05** | **Mental Arithmetic & Splitting**<br>*(5 min)* | • **Splitting:** $47 \times 6 = (40 \times 6) + (7 \times 6) = 240 + 42 = 282$.<br>• **Addition:** $38 + 59 + 27 = 124$.<br>• **Percentage Inversion:** $68\%$ of $250 = 250\%$ of $68 = 68 \times 2.5 = 170$.<br>• **Base 100 Multiplications:** $96 \times 93 = 8928$. | One-line rough work or pure mental calculation. Train brain to decompose numbers into clean tens and units. |

> **தமிழில்:** அட்டவணையை வரிசையாகச் சொல்வது மட்டும் போதாது. கலந்த வரிசையில் கேட்கும்போதும் விடை தெரிய வேண்டும். வர்க்கம்–வர்க்கமூலம், கனம்–கனமூலம் ஆகியவற்றை இரு திசைகளிலும் பயிற்சி செய்.

### 4.1 What One Useful Word Entry Looks Like

> **Example:**  
> **Brief — சுருக்கமான — short in length or duration.**  
> *Usage:* “She gave a brief explanation of the new policy.”  
> *Related words:* **concise** (synonym); **lengthy** (antonym).  
> *Standard:* Meanings and opposites depend on context. One clear sentence is enough.

### 4.2 Weekly Reading & Grammar Rotation
- **Monday / Wednesday / Friday:** Read one 150–200 word paragraph $\to$ 1-sentence summary.
- **Tuesday / Thursday / Saturday:** Solve 3–5 questions on a learned grammar rule (Subject-Verb Agreement, Pronouns, Tenses).
- **Sunday:** Review week's errors inside existing Sunday revision slot; do not add extra editorial tasks.

> **தமிழில்:** புதிய சொற்களைவிட பழைய சொற்கள் நினைவில் உள்ளனவா என்பதே முதலில் முக்கியம். ஒரு சொல்லின் தமிழ் அர்த்தத்துடன் அது ஆங்கில வாக்கியத்தில் எப்படி பயன்படுகிறது என்பதையும் அறிந்துகொள்.

### 6.1 Concrete Retrieval Examples (Static & Current Affairs)
- **Static Science Recall:**
  - Which metal is at the centre of chlorophyll? **Magnesium.**
  - Which plant tissue carries organic food? **Phloem.**
  - What does a blue-black iodine result indicate? **Starch.**
  - Key distinction: **chloroplast = organelle; chlorophyll = pigment**.
- **Current Affairs Recall:**
  - Record: **event date**, **what happened**, and **person/place/organization**.
  - Always separate the event's actual date from the article's publication date.

> **தமிழில்:** குறிப்பைப் பார்த்துக்கொண்டே படிப்பது நினைவில் இருப்பதற்குச் சான்றல்ல. மூடிவிட்டு பதில் சொல்; பிறகு சரிபார். நாள்தோறும் புதிய அத்தியாயம் தொடங்காமல், படித்தவற்றை மீட்டெடு.

## 8. 🔁 The Spaced Retrieval Engine & Zero-Backlog Rules

**Recall first, then check.** Returning to material after gaps and trying to retrieve it builds enduring retention; passive rereading creates an illusion of competence ([AERO Spacing & Retrieval Guide](https://www.edresearch.edu.au/guides-resources/practice-guides/spacing-and-retrieval-practice-guide-full-publication)).

### 8.1 Spaced Review Cadence

| When | Core Action | Execution Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Same Session** | Immediate retrieval | Cover the answer, attempt once from memory, then verify. |
| **Next Day** | Overnight consolidation | Re-test yesterday's facts and errors without looking at notes. |
| **Day 3** | Queue reinforcement | Review weak items and new vocabulary words. |
| **Day 7** | Domain mixing | Mix with older items; perform unaided chapter error re-solves. |
| **Day 21** | Long-term test | Check retention without any hints or context clues. |

### 8.2 The 5 Zero-Backlog Safety Rules
1. **Review due errors before adding any new facts.**
2. **Reduce new words/cards to ZERO temporarily** if the review queue exceeds the timebox.
3. **Stop strictly at the slot limit**; note what remains due and resume tomorrow.
4. **Remove duplicate or low-value cards** and split multi-part questions into single-fact prompts.
5. **Resume after a missed day without doubling tomorrow's study hours.**

### B. Core Principles of Progression:
1. **Frictionless Arithmetic in Maths:**
   - Instant recall of squares ($1^2-50^2$), cubes ($1^3-30^3$), and reciprocal percentage conversions ($1/2$ through $1/20$) removes cognitive load during multi-step Algebra, Geometry, and Arithmetic problems.
2. **Grammar & Vocabulary Intuition in English:**
   - Reviewing 3–5 words daily with sentence application builds passive vocabulary into active recognition for Cloze Tests, Synonyms/Antonyms, and Reading Comprehension.

   - Knowing letter values ($A = 1 \dots Z = 26$) and opposites ($27 - p$) instantly turns Coding-Decoding, Analogy, and Series into 15-second mechanical points.

   - 55 minutes of daily spaced recall beats 5 hours of weekend cramming every single time.
