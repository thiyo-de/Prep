# English: Detailed Subject Checklist

[Index and completion rules](README.md) | [Master plan](../FINAL-PLAN-NOV2026-MAY2027.md)

**Existing allocation:** 90 minutes: 20 vocabulary + 30 lesson/notes + 40 practice/corrections.

English is the SSC part of the combined plan. Start with simple sentences, introduce reading gradually, and add cloze after the necessary grammar. The first grammar pass is not a mastery deadline. During the main routine, learn 10 new words daily and review after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days; follow the master plan for its separate head-start allocation. Write one correct sentence per word.

Tick a skill after independent practice without notes. The chapter-progress boxes track testing and retention separately. Book codes identify chapters, not study-order numbers. Follow the order below; dates remain in the master plan.

## EF1 — English basics: sentences and parts of speech

- [ ] Review letters, vowels and consonants if needed
- [ ] Distinguish a word, phrase and complete sentence
- [ ] Identify subject, verb and object in simple sentences
- [ ] Use capitals and basic punctuation
- [ ] Recognise nouns and pronouns in suitable sentences
- [ ] Recognise verbs, adjectives and adverbs
- [ ] Recognise prepositions, conjunctions and interjections
- [ ] Explain how a word's role can change with its sentence

**Foundation check**

- [ ] Explain the basics without notes and complete easy exercises independently.
- [ ] Record easy-set accuracy; reach at least 70% before harder application.
- [ ] Log errors and revisit them after 7 and 21 days.
