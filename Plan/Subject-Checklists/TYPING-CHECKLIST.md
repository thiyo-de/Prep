# Typing: Beginner-to-Exam-Practice Checklist

[Index and completion rules](README.md) | [Master plan](../FINAL-PLAN-NOV2026-MAY2027.md)

**Existing allocation:** 20 minutes during the workday lunch break, separate from English time. Keep time to eat. Begin with 10 minutes key accuracy + 10 minutes simple text; later use a 15-minute passage + 5 minutes error review.

## Common study rule — use for every stage

1. Learn the key positions or passage skill from a short demonstration.
2. Practise slowly on fresh text until the movement and spacing are reliable.
3. Record repeated mistakes and practise the weak keys/words.
4. Increase passage length, then speed, while keeping accuracy stable. The 70% MCQ gate does not apply to typing.
5. Compare three unfamiliar passages using the same duration and scoring tool. Record output, gross WPM, accuracy/errors and net WPM where available.
6. Revisit error patterns on fresh text after 7 and 21 days. If three comparable attempts show no improvement, use the existing repair time.

Tick a subtopic after demonstrating it independently. Continue practice after ticking; a memorised passage does not establish unseen-passage speed. Stage numbers are learning labels, not book chapter codes.

## Stage 4 — Timed passages

- [ ] Full 15-minute practice passages
- [ ] Accuracy and speed together under time pressure
- [ ] Gross speed, errors and net speed using a consistent scoring method
- [ ] Master plan's 35–40 English WPM practice goal with high accuracy

**Stage record:** three comparable attempts __ | speed/accuracy trend __ | next repair __

## Stage 5 — Exam-format data entry

- [ ] Applicable notice: duration, output measure and error rules
- [ ] Permitted editing tools and test settings
- [ ] Fresh passages under the applicable exam conditions
- [ ] Output and error calculation using that exam's scoring method
- [ ] Required standard demonstrated across three recorded attempts

**Stage record:** exam/cycle/rule reference __ | three results __ | remaining gap __

The WPM goal is a practice target, not an official qualifying guarantee. SSC data entry and post-specific typing formats are not interchangeable. Railway skill requirements depend on the applicable notification; do not assume an old cycle's typing test applies to every future cycle.
