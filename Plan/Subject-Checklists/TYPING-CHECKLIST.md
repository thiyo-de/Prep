## 📋 Universal 6-Step Typing Practice Protocol

1. **Posture & Finger Placement:** Sit upright; place index fingers on home keys (F and J notches). Never look at the keyboard.
2. **Slow, Deliberate Accuracy:** Type unfamiliar drills slowly until movements are completely automatic. Accuracy precedes speed.
3. **Log Weak Bigrams & Keys:** Record keys causing hesitations or typos (e.g., `b`, `p`, numbers, special symbols) in your error log.
4. **Gradual Pacing Increase:** Increase passage length before pushing raw speed. Maintain smooth, unhurried cadence.
5. **Standardized Passage Testing:** Evaluate 3 unseen passages under identical software settings. Log gross WPM, errors, and net WPM.
6. **Error Review (7 & 21 Days):** Re-test previously failed bigrams and passage styles after **7 days** and **21 days**.

> 💡 **Ticking Rule:** Tick a subtopic (`- [ ]` → `- [x]`) only after independently demonstrating the benchmark on unseen text. Continuous practice must continue even after ticking.
