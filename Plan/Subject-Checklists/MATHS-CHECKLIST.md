# Maths: Detailed Subject Checklist

[Index and completion rules](README.md) | [Master plan](../FINAL-PLAN-NOV2026-MAY2027.md)

**Existing allocation:** 120 minutes: 40 lesson/examples/notes + 60 solving + 20 error revision. Use 5 minutes inside lesson time for arithmetic recall.

Foundations are prerequisites, not a demand to memorise every table before Number System. Continue recall alongside chapters. Partnership/ages are introduced alongside ratio/mixture; keep their completion record under M24. M22/M23 share statistics: learn once, practise both sets.

Tick a skill after independent practice without notes. The chapter-progress boxes track testing and retention separately. Book codes identify chapters, not study-order numbers. Follow the order below; dates remain in the master plan.
