### 🏛️ The Four Pillars of Reasoning Deduction

| Pillar | Core Skill & Demonstration Requirement |
|:---|:---|
| 🧠 **1. Analytical Concepts** | Explain the underlying pattern, rule, or method; understand why the correct answer fits and why alternatives fail. |
| 📖 **2. Vocabulary & Instructions** | Accurately decode tricky qualifiers: *only, except, neither/nor, at least, at most, definitely true vs. possible*. |
| 🌍 **3. Background & Factual Associations** | Master real-world relationships for analogies/classifications (tools $\leftrightarrow$ workers, instruments $\leftrightarrow$ units, diseases $\leftrightarrow$ organs). |
| ⏱ **4. PYQ Practice & Tests** | Progress from untimed analysis to timed sectionals. Analyze skipped, slow, and guessed items. |

### 🏷️ Error Classification & Immediate Repair

| Error Type | Root Cause Analysis | Mandatory Repair Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 🧠 **Concept Gap** | Did not understand the logic, pattern, or constraint tracking method. | Review lesson video/notes; solve 5 easy examples independently. |
| 📖 **Instruction / Vocab** | Misunderstood a crucial qualifier (*at least, cannot be true*). | Note exact phrasing trap in error notebook; write meaning in own words. |
| 🌍 **Knowledge Gap** | Understood relationship structure but lacked specific factual knowledge. | Add fact to quick-revision sheet; review at 7/21 days. |
| ⏱ **Execution / Rushing** | Setup was sound, but made a counting slip, misread options, or rushed under timer. | Pinpoint the exact slip; retry with a short 2-minute timer. |

### 📋 Standard 6-Step Study Protocol

1. **Understand:** Watch video lesson + note down the core pattern/method and one worked diagram.
2. **Setup:** Draw a clean diagram (family tree, direction compass, seating circle, Venn diagram) before solving.
3. **Easy Gate:** Solve 10–15 easy textbook questions. **Reach $\ge$ 70% unaided** before proceeding.
4. **PYQ Practice:** Attempt chapter PYQs independently before opening solutions; mark assisted items.
5. **Timed Test:** Take weekly Saturday timed sectional; record net score and accuracy in error notebook.
6. **Double Spaced Re-Solve:** Re-solve errors without notes at **Day 7** and **Day 21**.
