## How to tick

1. Open the chapter assigned by the master plan. Codes match the book list; the displayed order follows learning prerequisites.
2. Tick a skill after you can explain or apply it independently without notes. Watching a video alone is not completion.
3. Follow the common study rule at the top of each subject. Use each chapter's short progress record for the easy set, timed result and 7/21-day reviews; put detailed attempts in your error notebook. These happen inside existing study blocks.
4. Count assisted answers separately. Record correct/wrong/skipped, time and net marks where applicable. Accuracy alone can hide a low completion rate.
5. Re-solve each error after 7 and 21 days from the error date. If no errors occurred, use a fresh short sample to check retention and record that instead. Failed reviews return to repair; do not tick them merely because the date passed.

The checkboxes list subtopics and rules, in beginner-to-advanced learning order. Introductory recognition and later application are labelled separately. Foundations use explanation and easy exercises, not advanced PYQs. Arithmetic recall continues alongside chapters. Typing uses practical accuracy/speed records rather than MCQ gates. G12 points to the computer checklist so you do not repeat its assessments.

## Status rules from the current plan

| Evidence | Meaning/action |
|---|---|
| At least 70% on an unaided easy set | Ready to attempt harder chapter questions; not full mastery |
| Red: timed result under 70% or repeated concept errors | Repair in the next two practice blocks before the next new chapter in that subject; revisit every 7 days |
| Amber: timed result 70–79% | Targeted repair and revisit every 10 days |
| Green: timed result at least 80% AND both clean unaided reviews | Revisit every 3 weeks through mixed practice |

A timed result of 80%+ with reviews pending is **retention pending**, not Green. Keep other subjects moving during repair. Follow the master plan's dated targets and buffers. Chapter status does not establish whole-exam readiness.

The subtopics expand the existing plan and supplied checklist into learning tasks; they are not quoted official syllabus wording or predictions of chapter weightage. Current affairs, vocabulary and typing remain ongoing after an initial pass. Use the master plan for exam-format/source checks.


## Scope and source check

These checklists support **CGL Tier-I + Tier-II Paper-I and NTPC Graduate CBT-1 + CBT-2**, with CHSL backup preparation as defined in the master plan. Specialist CGL papers, technical railway exams and CBAT preparation are outside this scope. Shared academic coverage does not replace each exam's mocks or post-specific skill requirements.

Content was compared on **24 October 2026** with the syllabus sections of the [SSC CGL 2026 notice](https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_adv_cgl_2026.pdf), [SSC CHSL 2026 notice](https://ssc.gov.in/api/attachment/uploads/masterData/NoticeBoards/Notice_of_adv_chsl_2026.pdf), and the [previously supplied NTPC CEN 06/2026 notice in repository history](https://github.com/thiyo-de/Prep/blob/b7c531f5a493d12e430a198e49cdb43987a4a3bd/NTPC/Notice/Notification.pdf), sections 13.1 and 13.2.1. The NTPC live official PDF could not be retrieved during that comparison; later amendments were not verified through it. These references do not confirm 2027 rules or dates.

Foundation lessons and subtopic groupings are teaching choices, not additional official papers. Keep basic statistics/probability for CGL Paper-I. Use relevant course/PYQ depth for supplementary book topics; do not expand them into unrelated specialist courses. Newly encountered PYQ variations belong under the existing chapter, not in a new timetable.

**After this content pass:** follow the current plan. Reopen its structure only for an official requirement change or a demonstrated learning problem. Use weekly results to repair specific topics within existing time.
