## 2. Standard Ticking Rules

1. **Prerequisite Order:** Open the chapter assigned by the master plan. Codes match the printed book list; the displayed order follows learning prerequisites.
2. **True Independent Application:** Tick a skill **only** after you can explain or solve questions independently without checking notes or hints. Watching a lecture video alone is *never* completion.
3. **Short Progress Record:** Use each chapter's short progress header for the easy set accuracy, timed test score, and Day 7 / 21 review dates. Detailed working goes into your error notebook.
4. **Assisted vs. Unaided:** Count assisted answers separately. Record attempted, correct, wrong, skipped, and net marks. High accuracy on a small subset can mask a poor completion rate.
5. **Spaced Re-Solve Rule:** Re-solve each error at **7 days** and **21 days** post-mistake. Failed reviews return to repair; never tick a review item merely because the date has passed.

> [!NOTE]
> The subtopics expand the master plan into concrete micro-tasks. They are pedagogical learning steps, not official syllabus text. Current affairs, vocabulary, and typing remain ongoing after an initial pass.

## 3. RAG Status Matrix

| Health Status | Test Trigger & Criteria | Mandatory Action Protocol |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| 🟢 **Green** | **$\ge$ 80%** on timed test **AND** 2 clean unaided re-solves | Revisit every **3 weeks** via mixed chapter sets. |
| 🟡 **Amber** | **70% – 79%** on timed test | Re-solve 10 targeted PYQs; revisit every **10 days**. |
| 🔴 **Red** | **< 70%** on timed test / repeated concept errors | **Hard Stop:** Dedicate next 2 practice blocks to repair before starting next chapter; revisit every **7 days**. |

> [!WARNING]
> **Retention Pending:** Scoring 80%+ with Day 7 or Day 21 review unfinished is **retention pending**, not Green. Complete both clean unaided reviews first.
