# 📚 Unified Study Guides & Knowledge Base (`Guides`)

> **Architectural Purpose:** A single, centralized knowledge repository for all competitive government exam preparations (SSC CHSL, RRB NTPC, etc.).  
> Because **SSC** and **Railway** share ~85–90% of the syllabus (Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, and General Awareness), all core conceptual notes, study PDFs, and prompts are maintained here once to prevent duplicate notes across exam folders.

## 🔄 How Exams Map to These Guides

| Subject | Shared Syllabus Scope | SSC CHSL Focus | RRB NTPC Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quantitative Aptitude** | Number Systems, Percentages, Ratio, SI/CI, Profit & Loss, Time-Work, Speed-Distance, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry. | High calculation speed (**36 sec/Q**), higher weightage on Advanced Maths (Geometry, Trig). | Conceptual calculation (**54 sec/Q**), heavy focus on Arithmetic & Elementary Statistics. |
| **Reasoning** | Analogy, Classification, Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Syllogisms, Venn Diagrams, Puzzles. | Fast pattern recognition (<15 sec), non-verbal / figural reasoning. | Puzzle-heavy, linear arrangements, identical logical foundation. |
| **General Awareness** | History, Polity, Geography, Economics, Static GK, Current Affairs, General Science. | Broad General Studies (Polity, History, Art & Culture) + Current Affairs. | **High General Science depth (~40% of GA)** (Physics, Chemistry, Biology). |
| **English Language** | Grammar, Vocabulary, Voice/Narration, Cloze test, Reading Comprehension. | **Heavily tested** (25 Qs Tier-I, 40 Qs Tier-II). | **Not tested (0%)**. |
