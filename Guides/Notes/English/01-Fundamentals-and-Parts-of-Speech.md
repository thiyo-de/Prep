# 🏗️ PART I: THE GRAMMAR HIERARCHY (இலக்கணப் படிநிலை அமைப்பு)

In English, thoughts are assembled through a strict hierarchy of linguistic building blocks:

$$\mathbf{Letters}\ \longrightarrow\ \mathbf{Words}\ \longrightarrow\ \mathbf{Phrases}\ \longrightarrow\ \mathbf{Clauses}\ \longrightarrow\ \mathbf{Sentences}$$

```
1. Letters (எழுத்துக்கள்)     ──► 26 Alphabet symbols (A, B, C...)
2. Words (சொற்கள்)           ──► Meaningful combination of letters (Cat, Read, Fast)
3. Phrases (சொற்றொடர்கள்)     ──► Group of words WITHOUT a Subject + Verb pair (in the corner)
4. Clauses (உட்பிரிவுகள்)      ──► Group of words WITH a Subject + Verb pair (because he was ill)
5. Sentences (வாக்கியங்கள்)  ──► Complete independent thought starting with a Capital & ending in ./ ?/ !
```

### 1. CLAUSE (`உட்பிரிவு` / `துணை வாக்கியம்`)
* **What it is:** A group of words that contains **BOTH a Subject and a Verb**.
* **தமிழில்:** ஒரு **எழுவாய் (Subject)** மற்றும் ஒரு **வினைச்சொல் (Verb)** ஆகிய இரண்டும் ஒன்றாக இணைந்த சொல் தொகுதி.

```
                                    CLAUSE (உட்பிரிவு)
                                            │
                ┌───────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┐
                ▼                                                       ▼
INDEPENDENT / MAIN CLAUSE                               DEPENDENT / SUBORDINATE CLAUSE
(முக்கிய உட்பிரிவு / தனித்து நிற்கும்)                     (துணை உட்பிரிவு / தனித்து நிற்காது)
• Has Subject + Verb                                    • Has Subject + Verb
• Expresses a COMPLETE thought                          • Incomplete thought; starts with joining word
• Stands alone as a complete sentence                   • Cannot stand alone
• Example: "Ravi missed the train"                      • Example: "...because he woke up late"
```

* **Complete Combination:**
  $$\underbrace{\text{Ravi missed the train}}_{\text{Independent Clause}} + \underbrace{\text{because he woke up late.}}_{\text{Dependent Clause}}$$

---

### 2. PHRASE (`சொற்றொடர்`)
* **What it is:** A group of words that **DOES NOT have a Subject-Verb combination**. It provides only partial sense and acts as a single part of speech.
* **தமிழில்:** எழுவாயும் வினைச்சொல்லும் **இல்லாத**, முழுமையற்ற அர்த்தம் தரும் சொற்களின் தொகுப்பு.
* **Examples:**
  * *in the corner* (மூலையில்)
  * *at the railway station* (ரயில் நிலையத்தில்)
  * *with a red cap* (சிவப்பு தொப்பியுடன்)
  * *early in the morning* (அதிகாலை வேளையில்)

#### ⚖️ The Critical Difference: Clause vs. Phrase
| Feature | Phrase (சொற்றொடர்) | Clause (உட்பிரிவு) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Has a Subject?** | ❌ **NO** | ✅ **YES** |
| **Has a Verb?** | ❌ **NO** | ✅ **YES** |
| **Standalone Meaning?** | Incomplete sense | Can be complete (Main Clause) |
| **Example** | *in the dark forest* | *when they entered the dark forest* |

---

### 3. SENTENCE (`முழு வாக்கியம்`)
* **What it is:** A group of words that expresses a **complete, independent thought**. It must contain at least one Independent Clause, begins with a capital letter, and ends with a full stop (`.`), question mark (`?`), or exclamation mark (`!`).
* **தமிழில்:** முழுமையான அர்த்தத்தைத் தரும் சொற்களின் தொடர் வாக்கியம் ஆகும்.
* **Formula:** $\mathbf{Sentence} = \mathbf{Subject} + \mathbf{Predicate\ (with\ Verb)} + [\text{Complete Thought}]$

#### Sentence Types by Purpose / Function:
1. **Declarative / Assertive (செய்தி வாக்கியம்):** States a fact or thought (*The sun rises in the east.*).
2. **Interrogative (வினா வாக்கியம்):** Asks a direct question (*Where are you going?*).
3. **Imperative (கட்டளை / வேண்டுகோள் வாக்கியம்):** Gives a command, order, or request. Subject **"You"** is hidden/understood (*[You] Close the door! / Please help me.*).
4. **Exclamatory (உணர்ச்சி வாக்கியம்):** Expresses sudden, strong emotion (*What a beautiful painting!*).

#### Sentence Types by Structure (Crucial for SSC):
1. **Simple Sentence (எளிய வாக்கியம்):** Exactly **1 Independent Clause** (*Ravi plays cricket.*).
2. **Compound Sentence (கூட்டு வாக்கியம்):** **2 Independent Clauses** joined by coordinating conjunctions (**FANBOYS**: For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So) (*Ravi worked hard, **but** he failed.*).
3. **Complex Sentence (கலவை வாக்கியம்):** **1 Independent Clause + 1 or more Dependent Clauses** (*Although he worked hard, he failed.*).

---

### 4. SUBJECT (`எழுவாய்`)
* **What it is:** The person, animal, or thing that **performs the action**, or **who/what the sentence is about**.
* **தமிழில்:** செயலைச் செய்பவர் அல்லது வாக்கியம் யாரைப் பற்றிப் பேசுகிறதோ அவரே எழுவாய்.
* **How to find it:** Ask the verb: **"Who?" (யார்?)** or **"What?" (எது?)**
* **Examples:**
  * **The doctor** examined the patient. *(யார் சோதித்தார்? $\to$ **The doctor** = Subject)*
  * **Priya and her sister** study every night. *(Compound Subject)*
  * **Honesty** is a great virtue. *(Idea/Quality Subject)*

---

### 5. OBJECT (`செயப்படுபொருள்`)
* **What it is:** The noun or pronoun that **receives the action** done by the subject.
* **தமிழில்:** எழுவாய் செய்த செயலின் பலனைப் பெறும் சொல் செயப்படுபொருள்.

#### Direct Object (DO) vs. Indirect Object (IO):
$$\text{Subject} + \text{Verb} + \mathbf{Indirect\ Object\ (யாருக்கு)} + \mathbf{Direct\ Object\ (எதை)}$$

1. **Direct Object (நேரடிச் செயப்படுபொருள் — DO):** Directly receives the action. Answers: **"What?" (எதை?)** or **"Whom?" (யாரை?)**
   * *Example:* Ravi kicked **the football** (DO). *(எதை உதைத்தான்? $\to$ the football).*
2. **Indirect Object (மறைமுகச் செயப்படுபொருள் — IO):** Receives the Direct Object. Answers: **"To whom?" (யாருக்கு?)** or **"For whom?" (யாருக்காக?)**
   * *Example:* The teacher gave **Ravi** (IO) a **certificate** (DO). *(ஆசிரியர் **ரவிக்கு** (யாருக்கு?) ஒரு **சான்றிதழை** (எதை?) கொடுத்தார்).*

---

### 6. SUBJECT COMPLEMENT (`எழுவாய் நிரப்பி`)
* **What it is:** A noun or adjective that comes after a **linking / state verb** (*is, am, are, was, were, become, seem, look, taste*) and **renames or describes the SUBJECT**.
* **The Master Equality Rule:** A subject complement does NOT receive an action; it is the **exact same entity as the Subject**:

$$\mathbf{Subject} = \mathbf{Subject\ Complement}$$

* **Examples:**
  * Ravi is a **doctor**. *(ரவி ஒரு மருத்துவர் $\to$ "Ravi" மற்றும் "doctor" இருவரும் **ஒரே நபர்**!)*
  * She looks **happy**. *(அவள் மகிழ்ச்சியாகத் தெரிகிறாள் $\to$ "happy" என்பது எழுவாயை (She) விவரிக்கிறது).*

---

### 7. OBJECT COMPLEMENT (`செயப்படுபொருள் நிரப்பி`)
* **What it is:** A word (noun or adjective) that comes immediately after the Direct Object and **renames or describes that OBJECT**.
* **The Master Equality Rule:** It completes the object's identity:

$$\mathbf{Object} = \mathbf{Object\ Complement}$$

* **Examples:**
  * They elected **him** (Object) **President** (Object Complement).  
    *(அவர்கள் அவரை தலைவராக தேர்ந்தெடுத்தனர் $\to$ "him" தான் "President"!)*
  * The bad news made **Ravi** (Object) **sad** (Object Complement).  
    *(அந்த செய்தி ரவியை சோகமாக்கியது $\to$ "sad" என்பது ரவியை (Object) விவரிக்கிறது).*

---

### 💡 The Big Confusion Solved: Object vs. Subject Complement
* **Sentence A:** *Ravi bought a car.* $\longrightarrow$ Is "Ravi" equal to "car"? **NO.** (Therefore, *car* = **Object**).
* **Sentence B:** *Ravi is a teacher.* $\longrightarrow$ Is "Ravi" equal to "teacher"? **YES, same person.** (Therefore, *teacher* = **Subject Complement**).

# 🅰️ PART III: ARTICLES (`சுட்டுச்சொற்கள்` — A, AN, THE)

Articles are mini-demonstrative adjectives placed before nouns.

```
                                      ARTICLES
                                         │
                 ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                 ▼                                               ▼
INDEFINITE ARTICLES (A / AN)                          DEFINITE ARTICLE (THE)
(குறிப்பிட்டிராத சுட்டு)                                  (குறிப்பிட்ட சுட்டு)
• Speaks about ANY non-specific item                  • Speaks about a SPECIFIC, known item
• Used strictly with Singular Countable Nouns         • Used with Singular, Plural, & Uncountables
• "Bring me a chair" (Any chair)                      • "Bring me the chair" (That specific chair)
```

---

### 1. The True Rule of "A" vs. "AN" *(The Pronunciation Law)*
Never look at written letters (A, E, I, O, U). **Always listen to the spoken sound!**

| Article | Sound Required | Rule & Standard Examples | High-Frequency SSC Exam Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Use "AN"** | Before a **Vowel Sound** (உயிர் ஒலி: அ, ஆ, இ, ஈ, உ, எ, ஐ, ஒ...) | *an apple, an elephant, an ice-cream, an orange, an umbrella* | • **An hour** (silent 'H' $\to$ sounds like 'our'!)<br>• **An honest man** (silent 'H')<br>• **An heir** (வாரிசு — silent 'H')<br>• **An MLA / An MP** ('M' pronounced as "em" $\to$ vowel sound!)<br>• **An FIR / An NCC cadet** ('F' = "ef", 'N' = "en") |
| **Use "A"** | Before a **Consonant Sound** (மெய் ஒலி: க, ச, ட, த, ப, ய, வ...) | *a boy, a pen, a cat, a doctor* | • **A university** (starts with 'Yu' / 'யு' consonant sound!)<br>• **A European** (starts with 'Yu' consonant sound)<br>• **A one-rupee note** (starts with 'Wa' / 'வ' consonant sound!)<br>• **A union / A unique idea / A useful tool** |

---

### 2. When to Use "THE" (Definite Article)
1. **Unique Things in the Universe:** *The Sun, The Moon, The Earth, The Sky*.
2. **Rivers, Seas, Oceans, Mountain Ranges:** *The Ganga, The Amazon, The Arabian Sea, The Himalayas*.  
   *(⚠️ EXAM TRAP: NEVER use "the" before individual mountain peaks: e.g., Mount Everest, K2).*
3. **Holy Books & Renowned Newspapers:** *The Bhagavad Gita, The Quran, The Bible, The Hindu*.
4. **Superlative Degrees:** *The tallest boy, The best policy, The most difficult exam*.
5. **Musical Instruments:** *He plays the guitar, she plays the piano*.
6. **Repeated Noun (Second mention):** *I saw a boy. **The** boy was crying.*

---

### 3. Omission of Articles (Zero Article — எங்கு வரக்கூடாது?)
SSC tests these in **Spot the Error**:
* ❌ Before **Languages:** *He speaks English* (NOT *the English* — "The English" means English people!).
* ❌ Before **Sports:** *He plays cricket* (NOT *the cricket*).
* ❌ Before **Names of People/Cities:** *Ravi lives in Chennai* (NOT *the Ravi*, NOT *the Chennai*).
* ❌ Before **Routine Meals:** *We take breakfast at 8 AM* (NOT *the breakfast*).
* ❌ Before **Abstract Qualities in a general sense:** *Honesty is the best policy* (NOT *The honesty*).

# 📚 PART IV: THE 8 PARTS OF SPEECH (சொல் வகைகள்)

---

### 1. NOUN (`பெயர்ச்சொல்` — Naming Word)
* **Definition:** A Noun names a **person, place, thing, animal, quality, or idea**.
* **5 Types:**
  1. **Proper Noun:** Unique name (*Ravi, Delhi, India, Ganga*).
  2. **Common Noun:** General name of a class (*boy, city, river, book*).
  3. **Collective Noun:** Name of a group (*team, jury, crowd, herd, army*).
  4. **Abstract Noun:** Qualities/feelings you cannot touch (*honesty, love, courage, poverty*).
  5. **Material Noun:** Raw substance (*gold, iron, wood, cotton, water*).
* ⚠️ **SSC Exam Trap (Uncountable Nouns):** Words like *Information, Furniture, Advice, Scenery, Luggage, Poetry, Hair* are **never plural** and never take *a/an*.
  * ❌ *He gave me many advices.* $\to$ ✅ *He gave me **some advice**.*

---

### 2. PRONOUN (`பிரதிப் பெயர்ச்சொல்` — Replaces a Noun)
* **Definition:** Replaces a noun to avoid monotonous repetition.
* **Types:**
  * **Personal:** *I, you, he, she, it, we, they* (Subject) / *me, him, her, us, them* (Object).
  * **Possessive:** *Mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs*.
  * **Relative:** *Who, whom, which, that*.
  * **Demonstrative:** *This, that, these, those*.
* ⚠️ **SSC Exam Trap (Pronoun Case):** Prepositions (*between, except, but*) take **Object pronouns**:
  * ❌ *Between you and I.* $\to$ ✅ *Between you and **me**.*

---

### 3. VERB (`வினைச்சொல்` — Action or State Word)
* **Definition:** Expresses an action (*run, eat, write*), an occurrence (*happen*), or a state of being (*is, was, seem*).
* **Helping / Auxiliary Verbs:**
  * **Be forms:** *is, am, are, was, were, been*
  * **Have forms:** *has, have, had*
  * **Do forms:** *do, does, did*
  * **Modals:** *can, could, will, would, shall, should, may, might, must*
* ⚠️ **SSC Exam Trap (Subject-Verb Agreement):**
  * Singular subject = Singular verb (verb adds `-s/-es` in present tense).
  * ❌ *One of the boys are absent.* $\to$ ✅ *One of the boys **is** absent.* (Subject is "One"!).

---

### 4. ADJECTIVE (`பெயரடை` — Describes a Noun)
* **Definition:** Describes or qualifies a noun/pronoun (quality, quantity, color, size, number).
  * *Example:* He is a **brave** soldier. She wore a **red** dress. I have **two** pens.
* ⚠️ **SSC Exam Trap (Degrees of Comparison):**
  * Never use double comparatives or superlatives!
  * ❌ *He is more taller than Ravi.* $\to$ ✅ *He is **taller** than Ravi.*

---

### 5. ADVERB (`வினையடை` — Describes a Verb/Adjective)
* **Definition:** Modifies a **Verb**, an **Adjective**, or **another Adverb**. Answers: **How? When? Where? How often?**
  * *Manner:* *Quickly, bravely, slowly*
  * *Time:* *Yesterday, today, now, soon*
  * *Place:* *Here, there, outside*
  * *Degree:* *Very, extremely, quite, too*
* ⚠️ **SSC Exam Trap:** Do not use adjectives where adverbs are required!
  * ❌ *She sings beautiful.* $\to$ ✅ *She sings **beautifully**.*

---

### 6. PREPOSITION (`இடைச்சொல்` — Position & Relation)
* **Definition:** Placed before a noun/pronoun to show its relationship in **time, place, position, or direction**.
  * *Place:* *In, on, at, under, behind, between, among*
  * *Time:* *At 5 PM, on Monday, in 2026, since, for*
  * *Direction:* *To, into, towards, through, across*
* ⚠️ **SSC Exam Traps:**
  1. **Between (2 entities)** vs. **Among (>2 entities)**.
  2. **Redundant Prepositions:** Verbs like *enter, order, discuss, attack* do NOT take prepositions!
     * ❌ *He entered into the room.* $\to$ ✅ *He **entered the room**.*

---

### 7. CONJUNCTION (`இணைப்புச்சொல்` — Joining Word)
* **Definition:** Connects words, phrases, or clauses.
  * **Coordinating (FANBOYS):** *For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So*.
  * **Subordinating:** *Because, although, if, unless, while, since*.
  * **Correlative Pairs:** *Either...or, Neither...nor, Not only...but also, No sooner...than, Scarcely...when*.
* ⚠️ **SSC Exam Trap (Fixed Pairs):**
  * *No sooner* pairs strictly with **than** (NOT *when*).
  * *Scarcely / Hardly* pairs strictly with **when** (NOT *then*).

---

### 8. INTERJECTION (`வியப்பிடைச்சொல்` — Sudden Emotion)
* **Definition:** A word or sound expressing sudden emotion (Joy, Sorrow, Surprise, Pain), followed by an exclamation mark (`!`).
  * *Joy:* **Hurrah!** We won the cup!
  * *Sorrow:* **Alas!** His grandfather passed away.
  * *Surprise:* **Wow!** What an incredible catch!
  * *Approval:* **Bravo!** You played exceptionally well!

# 🔄 PART V: BASIC SENTENCE PATTERNS (S-V-O-C-A)

| Pattern | Code | Formula | Example Sentence | Detailed Breakdown |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Subject + Verb** | **SV** | $\text{S} + \text{V}$ | *Birds fly.* | Birds (S) + fly (V). |
| **2. Subject + Verb + Object** | **SVO** | $\text{S} + \text{V} + \text{DO}$ | *Ravi drives a car.* | Ravi (S) + drives (V) + a car (DO). |
| **3. Subject + Verb + IO + DO** | **SVIODO** | $\text{S} + \text{V} + \text{IO} + \text{DO}$ | *The teacher gave me a book.* | Teacher (S) + gave (V) + me (IO) + a book (DO). |
| **4. Subject + Verb + Complement** | **SVC** | $\text{S} + \text{V} + \text{SC}$ | *She is an engineer.* | She (S) + is (V) + an engineer (SC). |
| **5. Subject + Verb + Object + Complement** | **SVOC** | $\text{S} + \text{V} + \text{DO} + \text{OC}$ | *They elected him President.* | They (S) + elected (V) + him (DO) + President (OC). |
| **6. Subject + Verb + Object + Adjunct** | **SVOA** | $\text{S} + \text{V} + \text{DO} + \text{A}$ | *He kept the bag on the table.* | He (S) + kept (V) + the bag (DO) + on the table (Adjunct/Adverbial). |

# 🌟 PART VI: THE "GOLDEN SENTENCE" DEMONSTRATION

Here is a single sentence showing **all 8 Parts of Speech working together**:

$$\mathbf{Hurrah!}\ \ \mathbf{The}\ \ \mathbf{young}\ \ \mathbf{boy}\ \ \mathbf{and}\ \ \mathbf{his}\ \ \mathbf{sister}\ \ \mathbf{quickly}\ \ \mathbf{ran}\ \ \mathbf{into}\ \ \mathbf{the}\ \ \mathbf{house.}$$

| Word | Part of Speech | Grammatical Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hurrah!** | **Interjection** | Expresses sudden joy and triumph. |
| **young** | **Adjective** | Modifies and describes the noun (*boy*). |
| **boy** | **Noun** | Subject of the action (person). |
| **and** | **Conjunction** | Joins the two nouns (*boy* and *sister*). |
| **his** | **Pronoun** | Possessive pronoun replacing the boy's name. |
| **sister** | **Noun** | Compound subject (person). |
| **quickly** | **Adverb** | Modifies the verb (*ran*), answering *How?* |
| **ran** | **Verb** | Main action verb in past tense. |
| **into** | **Preposition** | Shows direction of motion into an enclosed space. |
| **house** | **Noun** | Object of the preposition (place). |

# ⚡ PART VII: 10-SECOND RAPID RECALL & EXAM TRAPS

1. **Clause vs Phrase:** A Clause has both **Subject + Verb**; a Phrase has **neither**.
2. **Subject Complement Equality:** In $\text{S} + \text{V} + \text{SC}$, the subject and complement are the **same person/thing** (*Ravi = doctor*).
3. **Object Definition:** An Object receives the action; answers **"What?"** or **"Whom?"** after the verb.
4. **Vowel Sound Rule:** **An** is used before **vowel sounds** (*an hour, an honest man, an MLA, an FIR*); **A** is used before **consonant sounds** (*a university, a European, a one-rupee coin*).
5. **Mountain Rule:** Use "The" for ranges (*The Himalayas*); NEVER for single peaks (*Mount Everest*).
6. **Uncountable Nouns:** *Information, furniture, advice, scenery, luggage* never take plural `-s` or article *a/an*.
7. **Preposition Pronoun Case:** After prepositions, use object pronouns: *Between you and **me*** (NOT *between you and I*).
8. **Preposition Redundancy:** Verbs like *enter, order, discuss, attack* do NOT take prepositions (*He entered the room*).
9. **Correlative Conjunction Pairs:**
   * *No sooner* pairs strictly with **than**.
   * *Hardly / Scarcely* pairs strictly with **when**.
10. **Single vs Two Sets of Yarns (Bonus):** Knitting uses **1 yarn**; Weaving uses **2 sets of yarns**.
