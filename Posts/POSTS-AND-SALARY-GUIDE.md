## Read the salary and score columns correctly

- **Basic pay:** starting fixed pay before allowances.
- **Gross/month:** basic + dearness allowance (DA) + house rent allowance (HRA) + transport allowance in the model below.
- **In-hand/month estimate:** gross minus modelled employee pension contribution and a placeholder for other deductions, **before any income-tax deduction**. Actual bank credit can be lower.
- **Gross/year:** 12 times monthly gross; excludes bonus, employer pension contributions and the value of benefits. It is not a CTC quotation.
- **OBC target score:** realistic cutoff range to safe target score based on recent CGL 390-mark Tier-II Paper-I and NTPC 120-mark CBT-2 patterns. In both exams, OBC cutoffs sit within 2–6 marks of UR; always aim for the higher safe target to secure home state / South Zone allocation (e.g., Chennai/TN).

All salary amounts below are in rupees. Monthly estimates are rounded to the nearest 100; annual estimates are in lakh.

## How the estimates were calculated

**A fixed illustration, not a forecast of 2027 pay:**

| Component | Assumption used |
|---|---|
| Basic | First pay cell for the stated level |
| DA | 60% of basic: verified January 2026 rate; not asserted to be the latest rate at joining |
| HRA | Low endpoint: 10% of basic; high endpoint: 30%. The middle city category uses 20%. Assumes HRA is payable and no government accommodation |
| Transport | Levels 3–7: 1,800–3,600 plus DA thereon; entry Level 2: 900–1,350 plus DA thereon |
| Employee pension deduction | 10% of basic + DA |
| Other deductions | Assumed 1,000/month for illustration, not an official fixed charge; replace with actual insurance, health/professional-tax and other deductions |
| Income tax | Not calculated: subtract actual TDS from the illustrated in-hand figure |

HRA and transport use separate city classifications. The endpoints show low/high combinations; they do not assign a salary to Trichy, Chennai or any guaranteed posting.

```text
DA = basic x 0.60
Gross = basic + DA + HRA + transport allowance including its DA
Illustrated in-hand before tax = gross - 10% of (basic + DA) - 1,000
Actual in-hand = gross - actual pension contribution - actual other deductions - TDS
Annual gross = monthly gross x 12
```

Example, Level 7 at the low endpoint: 44,900 + 26,940 + 4,490 + 2,880 = **79,210 gross/month**. Subtract 7,184 pension contribution and assumed 1,000 other deductions = **71,026 before income tax**.

Government accommodation can remove HRA and introduce licence-fee deductions. Bonuses, overtime, running/night-duty allowances, arrears and overseas allowances are excluded. Employer pension contributions and benefits are not monthly bank credit. Any future pay revision must be verified; no assumed pay-commission multiplier is included.

Allowance sources: [January 2026 DA order reproduced by EPFO](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Circulars/Y2026-2027/Circular_EnhancementOfDA_%2024042026.pdf), [DoE HRA order](https://doe.gov.in/files/circulars_document/7th_July_2017.pdf), [DoE transport allowance order](https://doe.gov.in/files/cenetral-pay_document/TPTA_Eng1.pdf), [PFRDA employee contribution reference](https://www.pfrda.org.in/en/web/pfrda/schemes/national-pension-system/unified-pension-scheme). The DA order was verified through official indexed text; direct PDF retrieval failed. The uniform model is a comparison tool, not a substitute for railway/department payroll rules.
