## 💰 How to Read the Salary Columns

```
Gross Salary  = Basic Pay + DA (60%) + HRA (10% to 30%) + Transport Allowance
Net In-Hand   = Gross Salary − NPS Pension (10% of Basic + DA) − Standard Deductions (₹1,000)
```

- **Starting Basic:** Entry cell basic pay under 7th CPC for that level.
- **Gross / Month:** Low end = Z-class cities (10% HRA); High end = X-class metro cities (30% HRA).
- **In-Hand / Month:** Estimated take-home credit **before income tax (TDS)**. Actual credit depends on housing allotment and tax slab.
- **Annual CTC Note:** 12× monthly gross excludes bonus, employer pension contributions, and medical benefits.

## 🧮 Score Architecture: SSC CGL Tier-II (390 Marks)

*Marking rule for scored objective sections: **+3 for Correct · −1 for Wrong · 0 for Skipped**.*

| Section / Module | Questions | Maximum Marks | Safe Target |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Section 1: Maths** | 30 | 90 | **78 – 84** *(26–28 Correct)* |
| **Section 1: Reasoning** | 30 | 90 | **80 – 85** *(27–29 Correct)* |
| **Section 2: English Language** | 45 | 135 | **115 – 125** *(40–42 Correct)* |
| **Section 2: General Awareness** | 25 | 75 | **35 – 45** *(14–17 Correct)* |
| **Total Scored Merit** | **130** | **390** | **315 – 335+** |
| **Section 3: Computer Knowledge** | 20 | 60 | **Qualifying Only** *(Min 25% / 15 Marks for OBC)* |
| **Section 3: Data Entry Speed Test (DEST)** | 2000 keystrokes | 15 min | **Qualifying Only** *(Max 25% error limit)* |

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendation

1. **Top Priority for Written Merit:** **CGL Level-7 ASO / Income Tax Inspector** offers the highest combination of starting authority (Level 7, ₹75K+ take-home) and pure written merit selection.
2. **Top Railway Priority:** **NTPC Level-6 Commercial Supervisor** offers Level 6 entry without requiring psychometric tests.
3. **Execution Law:** Focus on building Tier-II mastery; everything required for RRB CBT-2 and CHSL is a subset of this single high-scoring syllabus.
