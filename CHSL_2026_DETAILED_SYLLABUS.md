## 1. OFFICIAL SCHEME OF EXAMINATION

### A. Tier-I Examination (CBE) — [Para 13.8, Pages 16–17]

* **Mode:** Computer Based Examination (Objective Type MCQs).
* **Question Languages:** English, Hindi, and regional languages opted by candidate.
* **Marking:** $+2$ marks for correct answer.
* **Negative Marking (Para 13.8.2):** **$0.50$ marks** deducted for each wrong answer.
* **Sectional Timing:** **Strictly 15 minutes per subject** (Total 60 Minutes).

| Part | Subject (Official Title) | No. of Questions | Max Marks | Sectional Time Limit |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **I** | English Language (Basic Knowledge) | 25 | 50 | 15 Minutes |
| **II** | General Intelligence | 25 | 50 | 15 Minutes |
| **III** | Quantitative Aptitude (Basic Arithmetic Skill) | 25 | 50 | 15 Minutes |
| **IV** | General Awareness | 25 | 50 | 15 Minutes |
| | **TOTAL** | **100** | **200** | **60 Minutes** |

---

### B. Tier-II Examination (CBE + Skill Test) — [Para 13.9, Pages 17–21]

Conducted in **Two Sessions on the Same Day**:
* **Session-I (2 Hours 15 Minutes):** Sections I, II, and III.
* **Session-II:** Section-IV (Skill Test / Typing Test).
* **Marking:** $+3$ marks per correct answer.
* **Negative Marking (Para 13.9.11):** **$1.00$ mark** deducted for each wrong answer in Sections I, II, and III (33.3% penalty).

| Session | Section | Module | No. of Qs | Marks | Sectional Timing |

| **Session-I** | **Section-I** | **Module-I:** Mathematical Abilities<br>**Module-II:** Reasoning and General Intelligence | 30<br>30<br>**Total: 60** | 90<br>90<br>**Total: 180** | **1 Hour**<br>(30 mins for Maths +<br>30 mins for Reasoning) |
| | **Section-II** | **Module-I:** English Language and Comprehension<br>**Module-II:** General Awareness | 40<br>20<br>**Total: 60** | 120<br>60<br>**Total: 180** | **1 Hour**<br>(40 mins for English +<br>20 mins for GA) |
| | **Section-III** | **Module-I:** Computer Knowledge Test *(Mandatory, Qualifying)* | **15** | **45** | **15 Minutes** |
| **Session-II** | **Section-IV** | **Module-II:** Skill Test / Typing Test *(Qualifying)*<br>• Part A: DEO in Dept/Ministry (15,000 Key Depressions/hr)<br>• Part B: DEO Grade 'A' (8,000 Key Depressions/hr)<br>• Part C: Typing Test for LDC/JSA (35 wpm Eng / 30 wpm Hindi) | — | Qualifying | 15 Minutes<br>15 Minutes<br>10 Minutes |

## 2. TIER-I INDICATIVE SYLLABUS — [Para 13.10, Pages 21–22]

### 1. English Language [Para 13.10.1]
* Spot the Error
* Fill in the Blanks
* Synonyms / Homonyms
* Antonyms
* Spellings / Detecting mis-spelt words
* Idioms & Phrases
* One word substitution
* Improvement of Sentences
* Active / Passive Voice of Verbs
* Conversion into Direct / Indirect narration
* Shuffling of Sentence parts
* Shuffling of Sentences in a passage
* Cloze Passage
* Comprehension Passage

---

### 2. General Intelligence [Para 13.10.2]
Includes questions of both verbal and non-verbal type:
* **Analogy:** Semantic Analogy, Symbolic / Number Analogy, Figural Analogy
* **Operations:** Symbolic operations, Numerical operations
* **Classification:** Semantic Classification, Symbolic / Number Classification, Figural Classification
* **Series:** Semantic Series, Number Series, Figural Series
* **Pattern & Spatial:** Space Orientation, Venn Diagrams, Drawing inferences, Punched hole / pattern-folding & unfolding, Figural Pattern-folding and completion, Embedded figures
* **Cognitive & Logical:** Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, Emotional Intelligence, Word Building, Social Intelligence, Coding and de-coding, Trends, Other sub-topics (if any)

---

### 3. Quantitative Aptitude (Basic Arithmetic Skill) [Para 13.10.3]

* **i. Number Systems:** Computation of Whole Number, Decimal and Fractions, Relationship between numbers.
* **ii. Fundamental Arithmetical Operations:** Percentages, Ratio and Proportion, Square roots, Averages, Interest (Simple and Compound), Profit and Loss, Discount, Partnership Business, Mixture and Alligation, Time and distance, Time and work.
* **iii. Algebra:** Basic algebraic identities of School Algebra and Elementary surds (simple problems) and Graphs of Linear Equations.
* **iv. Geometry:** Familiarity with elementary geometric figures and facts: Triangle and its various kinds of centres, Congruence and similarity of triangles, Circle and its chords, tangents, angles subtended by chords of a circle, common tangents to two or more circles.
* **v. Mensuration:** Triangle, Quadrilaterals, Regular Polygons, Circle, Right Prism, Right Circular Cone, Right Circular Cylinder, Sphere, Hemispheres, Rectangular Parallelepiped, Regular Right Pyramid with triangular or square Base.
* **vi. Trigonometry:** Trigonometry, Trigonometric ratios, Complementary angles, Height and distances (simple problems only), Standard Identities like $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$ etc.
* **vii. Statistical Charts:** Use of Tables and Graphs: Histogram, Frequency polygon, Bar-diagram, Pie-chart.

---

### 4. General Awareness [Para 13.10.4]
* Questions designed to test the candidate's general awareness of the environment around him and its application to society.
* Knowledge of current events and matters of everyday observation and experience in their scientific aspect.
* Questions relating to **India and its neighboring countries**, especially pertaining to:
  * History
  * Culture
  * Geography
  * Economic Scene
  * General Policy
  * Scientific Research

---

## 3. TIER-II INDICATIVE SYLLABUS — [Para 13.11, Pages 22–24]

### 1. Mathematical Abilities [Para 13.11.1]
Includes all Tier-I topics with advanced depth, **plus Statistics & Probability**:
* **Number Systems:** Whole Numbers, Decimals, Fractions, Relationships between numbers.
* **Fundamental Arithmetical Operations:** Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Square Roots, Averages, Simple & Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Discount, Partnership, Mixture & Alligation, Time & Distance, Time & Work.
* **Algebra:** Basic algebraic identities of School Algebra, Elementary surds, Graphs of Linear Equations.
* **Geometry:** Triangle & its centres, Congruence & Similarity, Circle & its chords, Tangents, Angles subtended by chords, Common tangents.
* **Mensuration:** Triangle, Quadrilaterals, Regular Polygons, Circle, Right Prism, Right Circular Cone, Right Circular Cylinder, Sphere, Hemispheres, Rectangular Parallelepiped, Regular Right Pyramid.
* **Trigonometry:** Trigonometric ratios, Complementary angles, Height & Distances (simple problems), Standard identities.
* **Statistics and Probability [Para 13.11.1.vii — Tier-II Exclusive]:**
  * Tables and Graphs: Histogram, Frequency polygon, Bar-diagram, Pie-chart.
  * **Measures of Central Tendency:** Mean, Median, Mode.
  * **Measures of Dispersion:** Standard Deviation.
  * **Probability:** Calculation of Simple Probabilities.

---

### 2. Reasoning and General Intelligence [Para 13.11.2]
Verbal and Non-verbal questions with higher analytical and multi-step reasoning depth:
* Semantic Analogy, Symbolic operations, Symbolic / Number Analogy, Trends, Figural Analogy, Space Orientation, Semantic Classification, Venn Diagrams, Symbolic / Number Classification, Drawing inferences, Figural Classification, Punched hole / pattern-folding & unfolding, Semantic Series, Figural Pattern-folding and completion, Number Series, Embedded figures, Figural Series, Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, Emotional Intelligence, Word Building, Social Intelligence, Coding and de-coding, Numerical operations.

---

### 3. English Language and Comprehension [Para 13.11.3]
* Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms and their correct usage.
* Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Synonyms/Homonyms, Antonyms, Spellings/Detecting mis-spelt words, Idioms & Phrases, One word substitution, Improvement of Sentences, Active/Passive Voice of Verbs, Conversion into Direct/Indirect narration, Shuffling of Sentence parts, Shuffling of Sentences in a passage, Cloze Passage, Comprehension Passage.
* **Specific Comprehension Requirement (Official Notification Note):**
  > *"To test comprehension, two or more paragraphs will be given and questions based on those will be asked. At least one paragraph should be a simple one based on a book or a story and the other paragraph should be based on current affairs editorial or a report."*

---

### 4. General Awareness [Para 13.11.4]
* General awareness of the environment and its societal application.
* Current events and everyday scientific observation and experience.
* India and neighboring countries: History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Policy, Scientific Research.

---

### 5. Computer Knowledge / Proficiency [Para 13.11.5 — Mandatory Qualifying]
* **i. Computer Basics:** Organization of a computer, Central Processing Unit (CPU), input/output devices, computer memory, memory organization, back-up devices, PORTs, Windows Explorer, Keyboard shortcuts.
* **ii. Software:** Windows Operating system including basics of Microsoft Office like MS Word, MS Excel, and PowerPoint etc.
* **iii. Working with Internet and E-mails:** Web Browsing & Searching, Downloading & Uploading, Managing an E-mail Account, e-Banking.
* **iv. Basics of Networking and Cyber Security:** Networking devices and protocols, Network and information security threats (like hacking, virus, worms, Trojan etc.) and preventive measures.

| **Official Notification Para** | Para 13.8 & 13.10 | Para 13.9 & 13.11 |
| **Questions / Marks** | 100 Qs / 200 Marks | 135 Qs / 360 Marks (+ 15 Qs Computer) |
| **Negative Marking** | **$-0.50$** (25% penalty) | **$-1.00$** (33.3% penalty) |
| **Sectional Timers** | 15 mins per module | 30 mins / 40 mins / 20 mins / 15 mins |

| **Role in Final Merit** | Qualifying | **100% Final Selection Merit** |
