## HOW TO READ THIS FILE

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **#** | Original timetable number for Reasoning; local reference number for the other unnumbered subject lists |
| **Timetable Topic** | The practical exam topic name from the 90-Day Timetable PDF |
| **Syllabus Reference Category** | Editorial grouping in the detailed syllabus; not an official SSC category name |
| **Notification Topic / Mapping** | Named notification topics or an explicitly identified coaching-to-syllabus interpretation |
| **Match** | Direct = named syllabus topic; Mapped = inferred practice connection; Mixed = includes both; Supplementary = not separately named for that tier. |

**Mapped topics are preparation guidance, not exact official wording or proof of question frequency.** A broad topic label does not prove that every associated subtopic is covered by its lessons.

**Source version:** The timetable filename says 2026, but its cover says SSC CHSL 2025. Use its practice topics as a study aid; use the supplied 2026 notification for exam rules.

# EXAM TIMING AND QUALIFYING COMPONENTS

Standard timings below exclude applicable compensatory time.

| Component | Questions | Marks | Standard time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier-I: each of English, Reasoning, Maths and GA | 25 each | 50 each | 15 minutes each; 60 minutes total |
| Tier-II: Mathematical Abilities | 30 | 90 | 30 minutes |
| Tier-II: Reasoning and General Intelligence | 30 | 90 | 30 minutes |
| Tier-II: English Language and Comprehension | 40 | 120 | 40 minutes |
| Tier-II: General Awareness | 20 | 60 | 20 minutes |
| Tier-II: Computer Knowledge, qualifying | 15 | 45 | 15 minutes |
| DEO skill test, applicable route | Not applicable | Qualifying | 15 minutes |
| LDC/JSA typing test | Not applicable | Qualifying | 10 minutes |

- Tier-I marking: +2 correct / -0.5 wrong.
- Tier-II objective marking: +3 correct / -1 wrong. Final merit uses Sections I and II (360 marks), subject to qualifying the computer and applicable skill/typing components.
- DEO/DEO Grade A: 15,000 KDPH for departments/ministries in paragraph 8.1; 8,000 KDPH for other DEO/DEO Grade A posts. The split is department-based, not simply DEO versus Grade A.
- LDC/JSA typing: 35 WPM English or 30 WPM Hindi.
- For VH candidates with 40% or more visual disability, paragraphs 13.10.5 and 13.11.6 exclude Maps/Graphs/Diagrams/Statistical Data from the specified Maths and Reasoning components. Consult the notice for accommodations and exemptions.
