# CHSL + NTPC 2026 - All-in-One Covering List

One study reference combining both exam covering lists, their timetable topics and their additional syllabus coverage checks. Overlapping topics are grouped once; exam-specific requirements remain visible.

**Scope:** CHSL Tier-I and Tier-II; NTPC Graduate CBT-1 and CBT-2 in the supplied CEN 06/2026. Updated: 9 October 2026.

## How to use the list

Each subject has a completion checklist. Change `- [ ]` to `- [x]` when you finish the topic and all its items in the coverage table. In a GitHub repository file, use Edit to save ticks; preview checkboxes may not be directly clickable. A shared topic needs one tick, but practise the relevant exam formats separately.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| D | Directly named in the notification, or a clear equivalent label |
| M | Study subdivision or mapping to a broader official topic |
| S | Supplementary coaching/practice topic, not specifically named for that exam |
| II-D | Direct CHSL Tier-II requirement; not a separately named Tier-I requirement |
| - | No separate tested subject in that exam |
| D/M or M/S | The row combines items with different levels of source support; read the coverage column |

CHSL marks normally apply to both tiers unless stated otherwise. NTPC marks apply to both CBTs: the supplied notice lists the same syllabus for them. **D does not mean high weightage, and S does not mean impossible to appear.** NTPC's topic list is illustrative.

The source-row column traces the original opening timetable lists: **C** = CHSL, **N** = NTPC; **R** = Reasoning, **M** = Maths, **G** = General Awareness, **E** = English. For example, CR1 is CHSL Reasoning row 1. A dash means the topic comes from additional coverage checks, a daily schedule or the detailed syllabus.

The CHSL opening timetable list does not separately label **square roots, partnership, mixture/alligation, emotional intelligence or social intelligence**. Verify these in lessons or add them. English, CHSL Tier-II statistics/probability and qualifying components need their own preparation alongside the NTPC sequence.

Both coaching PDFs contain older cover labels: CHSL's cover says 2025; NTPC's cover says 2025 / 200-day strategy although its actual schedule is 120 days. Use their lessons as study aids, not as the authority for 2026 exam rules. The CHSL source skips Reasoning number 34; NTPC repeats Statement & Argument on one page, counted once here. NTPC page 24's shortcut-key column is labelled Maths but belongs to computer practice.

## 7. Exam-specific practice and qualifying requirements

| Component | CHSL | NTPC Graduate |
|---|---|---|
| First stage | Tier-I: 100 questions / 200 marks; 60 minutes; 15 minutes each for Maths, Reasoning, English and GA | CBT-1: 100 questions / 100 marks; 90 minutes; Maths 30, Reasoning 30, GA 40 |
| Second stage | Tier-II merit: Maths 30 Q/90 marks/30 min; Reasoning 30/90/30; English 40/120/40; GA 20/60/20 | CBT-2: 120 questions / 120 marks; 90 minutes; Maths 35, Reasoning 35, GA 50 |
| Objective marking | Tier-I +2 / -0.5; Tier-II +3 / -1 | Both CBTs +1 / -1/3 |
| Computers | Tier-II: 15 questions / 45 marks / 15 minutes; qualifying | Included in GA in both CBTs; not a separate computer paper |
| Typing / skill | LDC/JSA: 35 WPM English or 30 WPM Hindi, 10 minutes. DEO/Grade A: 15,000 KDPH for paragraph 8.1 departments, 8,000 KDPH for other routes, 15 minutes | Supplied CEN 06/2026 dispenses with CBTST for JAA/Senior Clerk; computer/typing proficiency is still expected and assessed after joining |
| Merit / further requirements | Tier-II Sections I and II total 360 marks; computer and applicable typing/skill components must be qualified | CBT-2 shortlisting, document verification and post-specific medical fitness; supplied process has no CBAT |

NTPC subject distributions are indicative and have no separate subject timers. Timings above are standard; eligible NTPC scribe candidates receive 120 minutes, and CHSL provides applicable compensatory time/exemptions. For CHSL VH candidates with at least 40% visual disability, paragraphs 13.10.5 and 13.11.6 exclude Maps/Graphs/Diagrams/Statistical Data from the specified Maths and Reasoning components.
