# 6. EXAM TIMING & MARKING — QUICK REFERENCE

## SSC CHSL 2026

| Component | Q | Marks | Time | Marking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier-I (all 4 sections combined) | 100 | 200 | 60 min total | +2 / −0.5 |
| Tier-II: Mathematical Abilities | 30 | 90 | 30 min | +3 / −1 |
| Tier-II: Reasoning | 30 | 90 | 30 min | +3 / −1 |
| Tier-II: English & Comprehension | 40 | 120 | 40 min | +3 / −1 |
| Tier-II: General Awareness | 20 | 60 | 20 min | +3 / −1 |
| Tier-II: Computer Knowledge (qualifying) | 15 | 45 | 15 min | Qualifying only |

- **Merit:** Sections I + II of Tier-II = 360 marks. Computer and skill/typing tests are qualifying only.
- **DEO skill test:** 15,000 KDPH (dept/ministry posts) or 8,000 KDPH (other DEO posts).
- **LDC/JSA typing:** 35 WPM English or 30 WPM Hindi.

## RRB NTPC Graduate 2026

| Component | CBT-1 | CBT-2 | Marking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 30 Q / 30 marks | 35 Q / 35 marks | +1 / −1/3 |
| Reasoning | 30 Q / 30 marks | 35 Q / 35 marks | +1 / −1/3 |
| General Awareness | 40 Q / 40 marks | 50 Q / 50 marks | +1 / −1/3 |
| **Total** | **100 Q / 100 marks / 90 min** | **120 Q / 120 marks / 90 min** | — |

- No English section. No CBAT or typing test for CEN 06/2026.
- CBT-1 = screening. CBT-2 = shortlisting for document verification.
