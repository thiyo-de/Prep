# RRB NTPC Graduate 2026

Pay-first post priorities and examination dates from the supplied CEN 06/2026. Updated: 12 October 2026.

## NTPC Graduate vs. Undergraduate recruitments

RRB conducts NTPC recruitment under **two separate notifications** based on educational qualification and pay scale:

| Parameter | NTPC Graduate Level (This Repository) | NTPC Undergraduate Level (12th Pass) |
|---|---|---|
| **Notification** | **CEN No. 06/2026** (supplied in `Notice/`) | Separate CEN notification |
| **Minimum Qualification** | **Any Bachelor's Degree (Graduation)** | **12th Standard (+2 stage)** or equivalent |
| **Pay Levels** | **Level 5 & Level 6** | **Level 2 & Level 3** |
| **Starting Basic Pay** | Rs. 29,200 to Rs. 35,400 | Rs. 19,900 to Rs. 21,700 |
| **Approx. Monthly In-Hand** | **Rs. 47,000 to Rs. 67,000+** | **Rs. 30,000 to Rs. 38,000** |
| **Key Posts** | Chief Commercial Supervisor (L6), Goods Train Manager (L5), Senior Clerk (L5), JAA (L5) | Commercial cum Ticket Clerk (L3), Accounts Clerk (L2), Junior Clerk (L2), Trains Clerk (L2) |

### Exam Stages for Both Tracks

Both tracks use a two-stage computer-based examination structure:

1. **CBT-1 (Screening Test):** 100 Qs | 100 marks | 90 minutes (Maths 30, Reasoning 30, General Awareness 40). Marks are used solely to shortlist candidates for CBT-2 (~1:15 ratio per vacancy).
2. **CBT-2 (Final Merit Test):** 120 Qs | 120 marks | 90 minutes (Maths 35, Reasoning 35, General Awareness 50). 
   - Graduate candidates write separate CBT-2 papers for Level 5 and Level 6.
   - For CEN 06/2026, the typing skill test has been dispensed with for clerical posts; CBT-2 normalized score is 100% decisive for final merit.

> **Candidate Dual-Eligibility:** Any candidate with a Bachelor's Degree is eligible to apply for **both** notifications. Because the syllabus (Mathematics, Reasoning, and General Awareness) is identical, preparing for the Graduate level automatically prepares you to clear the Undergraduate exam.

## Job priority and salary

| Priority | Post | Pay level | Starting basic / month | Approx. in-hand / month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor | 6 | Rs. 35,400 | Rs. 57,000-67,000 |
| 2 | Goods Train Manager | 5 | Rs. 29,200 | Duty-dependent; see note below |
| 3 (equal) | Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist | 5 | Rs. 29,200 | Rs. 47,000-56,000 |
| 3 (equal) | Senior Clerk cum Typist | 5 | Rs. 29,200 | Rs. 47,000-56,000 |

**Income-first preference: Commercial Supervisor > Goods Train Manager > JAA / Senior Clerk.** Commercial Supervisor has the highest starting basic pay. Goods Train Manager is placed second for running-allowance potential, not a guaranteed take-home ranking; actual earnings can exceed those of higher-basic-pay posts. JAA and Senior Clerk have equal starting pay.

Goods Train Manager pay depends on running duties, kilometreage and special running-staff pay rules. A reliable monthly in-hand estimate cannot be derived from this recruitment notice alone; the clerical salary range should not be treated as its payslip estimate. Operational shifts also affect preparation time.

Estimates assume 60% DA, 10-30% HRA, standard transport allowance, 10% pension contribution on basic + DA, and roughly Rs. 300-1,000 in other deductions. They exclude individual income-tax deductions, loans and special allowances. Government quarters, training pay and later allowance revisions can change the amount; these are planning estimates, not salary guarantees.

Calculation references: [DA baseline](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Circulars/Y2026-2027/Circular_EnhancementOfDA_%2024042026.pdf), [HRA rules](https://www.pib.gov.in/NEWSITE/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=3&relid=166967), [transport allowance](https://doe.gov.in/files/cenetral-pay_document/TPTA_Eng1.pdf), [pension contribution](https://www.pfrda.org.in/en/web/pfrda/schemes/national-pension-system/nps-for-central-government).

## Target scores and cutoff benchmarks

Target scores for selection (OBC / UR baseline; OBC cutoffs in NTPC are typically within 3-5 marks of UR). Typing skill tests for Senior Clerk and JAA have been dispensed with for CEN 06/2026, making CBT-2 scores 100% decisive for final merit.

### CBT-1: Screening targets (out of 100)

| Target post level | Safe raw score target | Expected normalized cutoff (RRB Chennai / SR) |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Level 6 (Commercial Supervisor)** | **75-80+** | 74-80 |
| **Level 5 (Goods Guard / Clerks)** | **68-74+** | 68-74 |

### CBT-2: Final merit targets (out of 120)

Merit ranking uses Mathematics (35 Q) + Reasoning (35 Q) + General Awareness (50 Q) = 120 marks (+1 / -1/3).

| Post | Pay level | Approx. in-hand | Safe merit target (out of 120) | Target score % |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor** | 6 | Rs. 57,000-67,000 | **94-100+** | ~80% (Highest zone competition) |
| **Goods Train Manager** | 5 | Rs. 50,000-65,000+ (with running allowances) | **86-92+** | ~73-76% |
| **Senior Clerk / Junior Accounts Assistant** | 5 | Rs. 47,000-56,000 | **83-88+** | ~70-73% |

### CBT-2 Sectional Breakdown for 90+ Target

- **Reasoning (35 Q):** 31-33 correct (~31-33 marks)
- **Mathematics (35 Q):** 30-32 correct (~30-32 marks)
- **General Awareness (50 Q):** 30-34 correct (~30-34 marks)
  - General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Life Science up to 10th CBSE) accounts for ~40% (~20 Qs) of GA and is the primary competitive differentiator.

## Posting

RRB Chennai / Southern Railway does not guarantee Trichy. Southern Railway postings can be elsewhere in Tamil Nadu or Kerala; ICF is a Chennai option. Location and duty hours are secondary considerations after pay in the preference order above.
