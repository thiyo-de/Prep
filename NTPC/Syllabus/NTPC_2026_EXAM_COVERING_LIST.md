## HOW TO READ THIS FILE

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| # | Local reference number assigned here; the timetable topic tables are not numbered. |
| Timetable Topic | Topic from the coaching timetable; abbreviations are retained where useful. |
| Syllabus Reference Category | Editorial category in the detailed syllabus, not an official RRB category name. |
| Notification Topic / Mapping | Named notification topic and, where needed, an explanation of the inferred connection. |
| Match | Direct = named topic; Mapped = practice subdivision of a broader topic; Supplementary = coaching topic without a specific named counterpart. |

**Mapped and supplementary rows are study guidance, not claims that RRB explicitly names those subtopics.** They do not establish exam frequency or guaranteed questions. The notification's list is illustrative (“etc.”), so absence of a specific name does not prove exclusion.

**Source version:** Although the filename says 2026 and the schedule says 120 days, the cover says “RRB NTPC 2025” and “200 DAY STRATEGY.” Use the PDF as a coaching topic/schedule source. Do not carry its vacancy figures, historical cutoff tables or older selection-stage claims into the current notification.

# CBT-1 vs CBT-2 — APPLICATION OF THIS LIST

| Component | CBT-1 | CBT-2 |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 30 questions / 30 marks | 35 questions / 35 marks |
| Reasoning | 30 questions / 30 marks | 35 questions / 35 marks |
| General Awareness | 40 questions / 40 marks | 50 questions / 50 marks |
| Total | 100 questions / 100 marks | 120 questions / 120 marks |
| Standard duration | 90 minutes | 90 minutes |
| Eligible scribe candidates | 120 minutes | 120 minutes |
| Negative marking | 1/3 mark per wrong answer | 1/3 mark per wrong answer |
| Listed official topics | Same topic list | Same topic list |

Subject distributions are indicative. The supplied notification specifies overall durations, not separate subject timers.

**There are no separately listed CBT-2-only syllabus additions in this notification.** Elementary Statistics and Computer Awareness apply to both stages. English is not a separate subject in either stage. Practise each exam format separately even though the topic lists match.
