# RRB NTPC 2026 (GRADUATE) — DETAILED SYLLABUS

> **Source:** [Supplied NTPC Notification — CEN No. 06/2026](../Notice/Notification.pdf), paragraphs 13.1 and 13.2.1, pages 19–20.
> **Scope:** Graduate posts covered by this notification; not the separate Undergraduate recruitment.
> **Format:** Subject-wise headings and topic lists, following the CHSL detailed syllabus format.
> **Editorial note:** Lettered categories group the notification's topics for easier study; they are not official syllabus category names. Broad topics are retained without adding unlisted coaching subtopics. The notification uses “etc.” and describes topics as likely areas, so these lists are not an exhaustive question boundary.

## CBT-1 vs CBT-2 — QUICK DIFFERENCE

| Component | CBT-1 | CBT-2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 30 questions | 35 questions |
| General Intelligence and Reasoning | 30 questions | 35 questions |
| General Awareness | 40 questions | 50 questions |
| Total questions / marks | 100 / 100 | 120 / 120 |
| Standard duration | 90 minutes | 90 minutes |
| Duration for eligible scribe candidates | 120 minutes | 120 minutes |
| Negative marking per wrong answer | 1/3 mark | 1/3 mark |
| Listed syllabus topics | Same topics | Same topics |
| Role | Screening for CBT-2 | Shortlisting for document verification |

**Important:** CBT-2 has more questions within the same overall duration. The identical topic lists do not imply identical question difficulty.

- There is no separate English Language section in either CBT.
- Basics of Computers and Computer Applications are included in General Awareness in both CBTs.
- Elementary Statistics is listed in both stages; it is not a CBT-2-only addition.
- The notice does not specify a fixed General Science percentage within General Awareness.

## MINIMUM QUALIFYING MARKS — BOTH CBTs

| Category | Minimum qualifying percentage |
| --- | --- |
| UR | 40% |
| EWS | 40% |
| OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) | 30% |
| SC | 30% |
| ST | 25% |

These thresholds also apply to Ex-Servicemen and PwBD candidates according to their community. The notification provides a relaxation of 2 marks for PwBD candidates if there is a shortage against reserved vacancies.

**These are minimum eligibility thresholds, not selection cutoffs.** Shortlisting depends on merit, applicable reservation and vacancies.

Source: paragraphs 13.1 and 13.2.2, page 20.

## OTHER SELECTION REQUIREMENTS IN THIS NOTIFICATION

- The stated recruitment process includes CBT-1, CBT-2, document verification and medical examination.
- The Computer Based Typing Skill Test for Senior Clerk cum Typist and Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist has been dispensed with for **CEN No. 06/2026**.
- Candidates for these posts are still expected to have basic computer and adequate typing proficiency. This is assessed after joining; unsatisfactory performance may affect or delay confirmation or promotion.
- The stated process does not list a CBAT stage. RRB reserves the right to conduct additional CBTs if considered necessary.
- Appointment remains subject to document verification, prescribed medical fitness and other appointment conditions.

Source: paragraph 13(b)–(c), page 19; paragraph 13.3, pages 20–21.
